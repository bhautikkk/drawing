How to Play DrawGo

New to DrawGo? Don't worry! It's incredibly easy to pick up, but mastering it takes creativity and quick thinking. Follow this guide to become a DrawGo champion.

The Basics

DrawGo is played in rounds. In each round, one player is chosen to be the Artist, and everyone else is a Guesser.

1. Join a Game

Enter your nickname on the main menu and choose "Quick Join" to hop into a public game instantly. Or, create a private room to play with friends and share the unique room code with them.

2. The Artist Draws

If you are the Artist, you will be given a choice of three words. Pick one! You then have a limited time (usually 60-80 seconds) to draw that word on the canvas. Warning: Writing letters or numbers is cheating!

3. The Guessers Guess

If you are a Guesser, watch the drawing closely. Type your guesses into the chat box. You can guess as many times as you like. The faster you guess the correct word, the more points you earn!

Scoring System

Points are awarded based on speed and accuracy:

  • First Guesser: Gets the maximum points for that round.
  • Subsequent Guessers: Earn slightly fewer points, decreasing as time goes on.
  • Artist: Earns points based on how many players successfully guess their drawing. So draw clearly!

Tools & Controls

As the Artist, you have a powerful set of tools at your disposal:

  • Brush Sizes: Use the slider to change the thickness of your lines.
  • Color Palette: Choose from a vibrant range of colors to make your drawing pop.
  • Fill Tool: Quickly fill large areas with color.
  • Undo: Made a mistake? Tap undo to step back.
  • Clear: Wipe the canvas clean and start over (use carefully!).

Tips for Success

  • Keep it Simple: You don't need to be Da Vinci. Simple shapes often convey the idea faster than detailed masterpieces.
  • Use Colors: A yellow circle is just a circle, but a yellow circle with rays is definitely the sun.
  • Watch the Hints: The game will show underscores `_ _ _` representing the number of letters in the word. Use this to filter your guesses.
